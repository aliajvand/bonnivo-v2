# Non-Goals (Out of Scope for Phase 1)

The following capabilities are deliberately excluded from Phase 1 to protect velocity, minimize regulatory exposure, and ensure hyper-focus on the core Commerce + Pet Daily Care MVP:

## 1. Veterinary Services & Medical Treatment
- **No Online Vet Consultations:** No video calling, audio chat, or tele-triage with veterinarians.
- **No Prescription Writing or Prescription Drug Sales:** Regulated pharmaceuticals requiring Rx are strictly prohibited from the catalog.
- **No Automated AI Medical Diagnosis:** The system will never claim to diagnose diseases, prescribe dosages, or replace qualified veterinary advice.

## 2. Service Provider Marketplaces
- **No Boarding or Daycare Booking:** Hotel reservation modules and live kennel camera streaming are out of scope.
- **No Grooming or Dog Walking Marketplaces:** Direct service scheduling and on-demand walker dispatching are deferred to future phases.
- **No Trainer or Behaviorist Directory:** Professional behavioral training booking is excluded.

## 3. Social & Community Mechanics
- **No Public Social Feed:** No algorithmic timeline, following/follower graphs, public commenting, or photo feeds.
- **No Public Unmoderated Review Engine:** Reviews are restricted to verified purchasers of specific orders with administrative moderation.

## 4. Complex Financial & Subscription Systems
- **No Automated Card-on-File Subscription Billing:** Iranian banking infrastructure does not natively support frictionless merchant-initiated recurring card billing without specialized direct debit contracts. Autoship is implemented via automated SMS replenishment reminders and one-click "Buy Again" flows.
- **No Multi-Currency Support:** All transactions, prices, and accounting are denominated strictly in Iranian Rials (IRR) / Tomans.
- **No Complex Seller Subscription Fees:** Seller revenue is strictly transaction-based commission.
