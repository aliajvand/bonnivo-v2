# Project Assumptions

## Assumption: Tehran Courier Buffer Covers Rate Fluctuations
Status: active
Date: 2026-09-30

### Assumption
Adding a 15–20% pricing buffer to estimated intra-city courier fees in Tehran will safely absorb peak-hour surge pricing from third-party couriers without eroding Bonyo's marketplace margin.

### Why
Third-party courier services (SnappBox, Alopeyk) exhibit price volatility during inclement weather and evening peak hours.

### How to Validate
Compare checkout-collected courier fees against actual disbursed courier receipts during the initial 50 orders in Tehran.

---

## Assumption: 4-Hour Post-Delivery Window is Sufficient for Discrepancy Claims
Status: active
Date: 2026-09-30

### Assumption
A 4-hour window following delivery confirmation is adequate for pet parents to report package damage, broken seals, or missing items, after which funds can be securely cleared for seller settlement.

### Why
Pet food and hygiene supplies are consumable; immediate inspection prevents fraudulent return requests after partial usage.

### How to Validate
Monitor customer support ticket volume and timing relative to delivery timestamps during Phase 1 pilot.

---

## Assumption: Weekly Catalog Excel Import Meets Seller Operational Capacity
Status: active
Date: 2026-09-30

### Assumption
Curated pet shop owners in Tehran are willing and able to provide an updated Excel/CSV inventory and price sheet once a week, while using a lightweight mobile seller interface for daily stock adjustments.

### Why
Traditional brick-and-mortar pet shops in Tehran do not have real-time webhook ERP integrations; Excel remains the ubiquitous standard.

### How to Validate
Onboard the first 5 pilot pet shops and track turnaround time and format compliance of their weekly catalog submissions.
