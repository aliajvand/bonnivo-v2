# Bonyo (بونیو) — Product Brief

## 1. Executive Summary
**Bonyo / Bonnivo** is an intelligent, Persian-first (RTL) pet ecosystem and marketplace designed to be the daily operating system for pet parents in Iran, launching with an initial pilot in Tehran.
Unlike traditional pet e-commerce stores, Bonyo is centered around the **"Pet Profile First"** principle: every product recommendation, daily routine, care task, reminder, and reorder trigger is anchored directly to the pet's distinct identity and lifecycle needs.

- **Persian UI Name:** بونیو
- **Technical & Code Identifier:** Bonyo / Bonnivo
- **Core Value Proposition:** "فروشگاه هوشمندی که پت را می‌شناسد" (The smart store that knows your pet).

## 2. Strategic Objectives (Phase 1)
1. **Pet Profile First Adoption:** Enable frictionless onboarding of multi-species pets (dogs, cats, birds, small pets) with health, dietary, and physical attributes.
2. **Daily Care Companion:** Deliver a personalized "Today" dashboard featuring species-specific daily tasks (e.g., dog walking progress, feeding, hygiene) and automated reminders.
3. **Curated Multi-Vendor Marketplace:** Provide a Canonical Product catalog model where multiple verified sellers compete on price and availability (Buy Box mechanism), with zero-inventory operations for Bonyo.
4. **Pet-Connected Commerce & Smart Reorder:** Allow cart items to be mapped to individual pets and calculate automated food replenishment alerts based on consumption rates.
5. **Safety & QR Pet Passport:** Generate tamper-resistant, owner-controlled QR pet passports with emergency lost-pet broadcasting.

## 3. Target Audience & Launch Geography
- **Primary Audience:** Urban pet owners in Iran seeking trustworthy nutrition, dependable care tracking, and convenience.
- **Initial Geography:** Tehran metropolitan area (intracity express courier delivery with scheduled delivery slots).
- **National Delivery:** Non-perishable items shipped across Iran via Iran Post and Tipax from day one.

## 4. Business & Monetization Model
- **Model:** Zero-Inventory, Curated Marketplace (Invite-only / Verified pet shops and importers).
- **Revenue:** Fixed 10% commission on completed and verified orders. Subscription fees are strictly out of scope for Phase 1.
- **Settlement:** 24–48 hours after customer delivery inspection window (4-hour return/damage claim window).
